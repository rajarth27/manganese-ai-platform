Manganese exploration and production planning

Real-time fusion of satellite multispectral imagery, Land Surface Temperature (LST), and dispatch fleet operational parameters for manganese mine management.

01

Where should we drill next?

Enter any coordinate in the Sausar belt and the platform pulls live Sentinel-2, MODIS and SRTM data for that exact point, then ranks it against the wider manganese belt. Use it to prioritise exploration near existing MOIL leases.

02

Will this shift hit target?

Feed in fleet availability, weather and delay figures for the coming shift. The model returns projected tonnage, a risk tier, and a SHAP breakdown naming which factor is costing you the most output.

03

What if we changed something?

Compare two operating states side by side — add trucks, reschedule blasting, model a monsoon week — and see the tonnage difference before committing equipment or capital.

How to read the outputs

What each number means, and what it does not
●

Prospectivity rank, not ore grade

Prospecting returns a percentile rank within the analyzed belt — a drilling priority order. It is not a manganese percentage, a tonnage estimate, or proof of a deposit.

●

Surface signals only

Optical satellites read the ground surface. Eight of MOIL’s eleven mines are underground and Balaghat works at roughly 383 m, so this maps host-horizon expression, not buried orebodies.

●

Risk tiers are calibrated

LOW below 10% shortfall, MEDIUM to 18%, HIGH to 30%, CRITICAL above. Bands match the model’s measured output range so every tier is actually reachable.
